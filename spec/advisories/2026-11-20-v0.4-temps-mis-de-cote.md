# Advisory 2026-11-20 — AgentBudget v0.4: refill time could be "set aside"

*English below · [Résumé en français](#résumé-en-français)*

| | |
|---|---|
| Affected | `contracts/agent_budget.sil` **v0.4** (testnet-10 only, never on mainnet) |
| Fixed in | **v0.5** (source hash in `contracts/EMPREINTES.txt`) |
| Impact | rate limit only: an agent could spend up to **3 × capacity within one refill period** instead of 2 × capacity. The vault balance, the per-payment cap, the allowlist and owner control were never affected. |
| Found by | our own loss-bound property test, while trying to prove the bound (not reported externally) |
| Reproduce | `./verify-proof.sh` (steps 2 and 3) |

## What happened

v0.4 refilled the budget from `lastDaa`, a time **announced by the agent**. The network only
guarantees that an announced time is not in the future (lock time), never that it is not in the
past. On a full, idle vault the agent could:

1. pay announcing `nowDaa = lastDaa`: no refill is claimed, the reserve is emptied, and `lastDaa`
   does not move;
2. immediately pay again with the current time: the whole idle period is credited at once
   (capped at one full reserve);
3. keep spending at the normal refill rate.

Measured by the real Kaspa script engine (`kaspa-txscript 2.1.0`): **3.00 × capacity** spent
within one refill period (test `faille_v04_demontree`).

## Fix (v0.5)

The refill now runs from `inputDaa`, the DAA score at which the network actually recorded the
vault, read in script with `OpTxInputDaaScore` (pattern from SompiVault by elldeeone). The agent
cannot choose it. An unconfirmed vault (score unknown) is refused.

```
int inputDaa = OpTxInputDaaScore(this.activeInputIndex);
require(inputDaa >= lastDaa);
int elapsed = clamp(nowDaa - inputDaa, 0, refillPeriod);
```

Same attack on v0.5: the set-aside time yields 0 and the spend stays at 2.00 × capacity
(test `attaque_temps_mis_de_cote_fermee`). Bound and proof: [`../borne-de-perte.md`](../borne-de-perte.md).
Long campaign: 4,000 scenarios per property test, against the real engine, all passed.

**Cost for an honest agent:** a few seconds of refill per payment, and a payment chained on an
unconfirmed vault waits about one second for confirmation.

## Migration

Vaults created with v0.4 must be repatriated by their owner (`vault-cli recuperer-v04`, which
compiles the archived v0.4 source in `contracts/archive/`) and recreated with v0.5.

## Status

The loss bound is proven on paper and verified by automated tests. It has **not** been
independently reviewed or audited yet. Testnet only.

## Résumé en français

En v0.4, la recharge courait depuis un instant annoncé par l'agent. Sur un coffre plein et
inactif, l'agent pouvait vider la réserve sans « consommer » le temps écoulé, puis réclamer ce
temps d'un coup : **jusqu'à 3 fois la réserve sur une seule période** au lieu de 2 fois.
Le solde, le plafond par achat, la liste blanche et le contrôle du propriétaire n'ont jamais été
touchés. Trouvé par notre propre test de borne. **Corrigé en v0.5** : la recharge court depuis
l'inscription réelle du coffre sur la chaîne (`OpTxInputDaaScore`). Vérifiable en une commande :
`./verify-proof.sh`. Non encore relu de façon indépendante ; testnet uniquement.
